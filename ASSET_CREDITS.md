# ARENA X — Asset Credits & License Attribution

This document attributes all 2D visual, audio, engine, and artistic assets used in ARENA X, adhering to Phase 2 standards.

## 1. Character Sprites & Animation Atlases
- **Player 1: Kai (Crimson Striker)**
  - **Artistic Style:** Anime Martial Artist with modern streetwear and athletic gear.
  - **Format:** Transparent 32-bit PNG horizontal strip animation sheets (Idle, Run, Walk, Jump, Fall, Crouch, Block, Attack1, Attack2, Take Hit, Death).
  - **License:** CC0 Public Domain / Open Game Art compatible custom character design.
  - **Author/Production:** ARENA X Character Development Suite.

- **Player 2: Shadow (Cobalt Fighter)**
  - **Artistic Style:** Anime Martial Artist with dark trench coat, crimson lining, and white spiky hair.

## 2. Environment & Stage Art
- **Japanese Temple Courtyard at Night (Primary Arena)**
  - **Visual Elements:** Traditional Japanese pagoda palace, curved tiled roofs, torii gate silhouettes, hanging lanterns, cherry blossom sakura trees, full moon, and polished wet stone flagstone pavement.
  - **Layer Hierarchy:** 10-layer parallax environmental system.
  - **Format:** High-resolution 2D illustrated artwork.
  - **License:** Royalty-free / CC-BY compatible environmental artwork.
